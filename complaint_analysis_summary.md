# Complaint Analysis Management Summary

## Executive Overview
This report provides an evidence-based summary of customer complaints processed across six primary categories. A total of **120 complaints** were analyzed in accordance with the standardized Week 2 metrics definitions.

## Key Findings & Volume Breakdown
* **Highest-Volume Category:** **Mortgage Servicing** represents the largest share of grievances with **31 complaints (25.83%)**.
* **Secondary Volume:** Card or Transaction Dispute follows closely with **25 complaints (20.83%)**.
* **Lowest Volume:** Account Access recorded the fewest instances with **9 complaints (7.50%)**.

## Resolution Performance (Closed Cases)
Resolution duration was calculated strictly using completed cases (`Closed` / `RESOLVED` status):
* **Fastest Resolution:** Service Quality averaged **6.17 days** across 6 closed cases.
* **Slowest Resolution:** Account Access averaged **16.20 days** across 5 closed cases.

## Limitations & Observations
* Unresolved or active cases (`Open`, `In Review`, `Escalated`) were excluded from resolution duration calculations to prevent time-skew distortion.
* Timestamp accuracy relies entirely on clean entry of `date_opened` and `date_closed`.
